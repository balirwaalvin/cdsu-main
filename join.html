Membership, Training & Institutional Tiers

Individuals, schools, associations, companies, and CSOs are welcome to join our subscription-based Career Development Programme or partner with regular termly/annual contributions.

Option 01 • Publication

Buy the Reference Book

UGX 50,000
Students, Parents, Teachers & Professionals

Get an original serialized copy of 'Career Development: A Path to Wealth' from our authorized distribution points.

  • Full 183-page manual + 3 Self-Assessment & Budget Annexures
  • Includes unique Book Serial Number to generate your CDP Access Code
  • Directly funds teacher rewards, school career clubs & youth outreaches
Option 02 • Adult Membership

Ordinary Member (Adults)

UGX 50,000
Parents, Employees & Out-of-School Youth

Full subscribing membership in the Career Development Programme (CDP) including 1 original copy of the book.

  • 1 Original Copy of 'Career Development: A Path to Wealth'
  • Access to CDP career seminars, work-life & retirement clinics
  • Eligibility for mentorship, networking & enterprise support
Option 03 • Student Membership

Ordinary Member (Students)

UGX 20,000
Primary, Secondary, TVET & University Learners

Subsidized student subscription for learners joining in-school or community Career Clusters (without personal book copy).

  • Enrollment in supervised school/community Career Cluster Clubs
  • Eligibility for Annual Career Skills-Exhibitions & competitions
  • Access to job-shadowing and holiday apprenticeship placements
Option 04 • Capacity Building

Subscribe for Training (T.O.T)

UGX 50,000
Youth, Parents, Career Teachers & Counselors

Specialized Training of Trainers (T.O.T), parent career clinics, and school counseling department facilitation.

  • Hands-on certification in administering the CDSU Career Syllabus
  • Training on Annexure 3 Career Cluster Scoring & Student Guidance
  • Peer benchmarking network with educators across Uganda
Option 05 • Institutional Package

Beneficiary School / College / Group

100 Books OR 250 Members
Schools, TVET Institutes, Universities & Churches

Procure a minimum of 100 books OR mobilize at least 250 ordinary members to unlock full institutional CDP services.

  • 20 Complimentary Books provided free to guide school training sessions!
  • Full setup & support of the school's Career Counseling Department
  • On-campus Career Fairs, Parent Forums & Exhibition qualification
Option 06 • Corporate & Sponsors

Corporate Member / Sponsor

UGX 1,000,000+
Companies, Banks, Universities, CSOs & Donors

Strategic partnership for organizations seeking talent recruitment, brand visibility, and national ESG/CSR impact.

  • Priority access to vetted protégés, interns & apprentices across clusters
  • Premium advertisement space in the book & website Sponsor Carousel
  • Access to CDSU Employment/Skills status reports & investment options
