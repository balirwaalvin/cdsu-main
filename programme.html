National Career Development Training Seminar

For Teachers and Career Counsellors. Equipping educators to guide Uganda's youth from education to skills, enterprise and wealth creation.

Dates19–21 January 2027
VenueSeeta High School, Mukono
Participants50 teachers & career counsellors
Registration feeUGX 50,000Terms & Conditions Apply.

Seminar objectives

  1. Introduce CDSU, its mission, objectives and national career development programme.
  2. Equip participants with practical tools to use the book Career Development: A Path to Wealth.
  3. Build skills to guide learners in career choice, employability, entrepreneurship and wealth creation.
  4. Establish a network of trained career development champions to implement the programme in schools and communities.
  5. Develop a pilot implementation plan with clear targets and reporting tools.

Key topics

  • Understanding the career landscape and education pathways
  • Self-awareness and subject-to-career mapping
  • Entrepreneurship and income generation
  • Employability, digital skills and the future of work
  • Career guidance, counselling and mentoring
  • Planning for implementation and monitoring

Key features

  • Interactive sessions and practical exercises
  • Practical guidance on education pathways, career options and entrepreneurship
  • Career exhibition and industry encounters
  • Development of institutional action plans
  • Group work, case studies, role plays and micro-teaching
  • Certificates of participation

3-Phase Methodology

How the Programme Works

Using our manual Career Development: A Path to Wealth as the lead reference, participants progress through three structured phases:

Phase A: Comprehensive Career & Wealth Seminars

Participants gain a deep, structured understanding of careers, self-awareness, and wealth creation through customized, thought-provoking sessions anchored in the 8 chapters of our manual.

  • Guided by the reference book 'Career Development: A Path to Wealth'
  • Covers Self-Awareness, Passion vs. Market Demand, Financial Literacy & AI Readiness
  • Tailored for In-School Students, Out-of-School Youth, Parents & Employees

Our 5 Core Products & Services

1

'Career Development: A Path to Wealth' Reference Book

Our 8-chapter, 183-page practical manual and workbook (ISBN 978-9913-678-02-5) featuring Uganda policy frameworks, TVET & University guides, B.E.S.T entrepreneurship tools, and self-assessment annexures. Includes a unique Serial Number for CDP access.

Order Book / Verify Serial
2

Training of Trainers (T.O.T) & Counseling Dept Support

We support schools and colleges to establish vibrant Career Counseling Departments, train career teachers and community facilitators, and conduct seminars for parents and adults.

Enroll for T.O.T
3

Career-Club Based Youth Innovation Facilitation

Hands-on facilitation of school and out-of-school Career Clusters—helping learners turn Competence-Based Curriculum projects into viable micro-enterprises.

Register a Career Club
4

Skills & Talent Enhancing Platforms

Regional and national Career Skills-Exhibitions, inter-club competitions, and peer founder networks that bring visibility to youth inventions and innovations.

Partner on Exhibitions
5

Job-Shadowing, Mentorship & Apprenticeship Linkages

Formal linkages between training institutions and private-sector employers to give participants real-world practice, mentorship, and apprenticeship pathways.

Host or Join Apprenticeships
